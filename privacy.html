Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority. Learn how we protect your data and what information we collect (spoiler: almost none).

🔒 No Data Collection. Guaranteed.

We don't collect personal information. We don't use tracking cookies. We don't sell data. Your privacy is protected by design.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lottery Pattern Analyzer. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Service.

Key Principle: Our Service is designed to run entirely in your browser. We don't collect personal data, and we don't send your information to our servers.

2. Information We Don't Collect

To make it absolutely clear, here's what we DO NOT collect:

3. Information Processed Locally

Our Service runs entirely in your web browser. Here's what happens locally:

3.1 Browser Storage

We use your browser's local storage to save:

  • Theme Preference: Your dark/light mode choice
  • Settings: Your preferred generator settings
  • Saved Numbers: Numbers you choose to save (optional)

Important: This data stays on your device. We cannot access it. You can clear it anytime using your browser's settings.

3.2 Temporary Processing

While using the Service, your browser temporarily processes:

  • Number generation algorithms
  • Statistical calculations
  • Heatmap visualizations

All processing happens in your browser and is discarded when you close the tab.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use minimal, essential cookies only:

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Theme Preference Remember your dark/light mode choice 1 year
Settings Storage Remember your generator preferences 1 year

No Tracking Cookies: We don't use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party tracking technologies.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete them. However, disabling cookies may affect some functionality of our Service.

5. Third-Party Services

We keep third-party services to an absolute minimum:

5.1 Web Hosting

Our website is hosted on standard web hosting services. These providers may collect basic server logs (IP address, browser type, access times) for security and maintenance purposes.

5.2 No External Analytics

We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other analytics services.

5.3 No Social Media Plugins

We don't embed social media widgets or plugins that could track you.

5.4 No Advertising Networks

We don't display ads, so there are no ad networks tracking your activity.

6. Data Security

We implement security measures designed to protect your information:

6.1 Technical Measures

  • HTTPS Encryption: All communication is encrypted
  • Security Headers: Modern web security headers implemented
  • No Data Transmission: Your data doesn't leave your browser

6.2 Organizational Measures

  • Privacy by Design: Built with privacy as a core principle
  • Minimal Data: Collect only what's absolutely necessary
  • Transparency: Clear communication about data practices

Important: While we take reasonable security measures, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

7.1 Access and Control

Since we don't collect personal data, there's no personal information for us to provide, modify, or delete. However, you can:

  • Clear your browser's local storage to remove saved settings
  • Use private/incognito browsing mode
  • Disable cookies in your browser settings

7.2 Data Portability

You can export any numbers you generate using our built-in export feature. The data is saved as a JSON file on your device.

7.3 Right to Be Forgotten

Since we don't store your personal data, there's nothing for us to delete from our servers. Any data stored locally on your device can be cleared through your browser settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 18 ("Children").

We do not knowingly collect personal information from Children. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Since our Service involves lottery number generation, it is intended for adults of legal gambling age in their jurisdiction.

9. International Data Transfers

Since we don't collect personal data, there are no international data transfers to worry about.

Our Service is accessible worldwide, but all data processing happens locally in your browser. No personal information crosses international borders.
