Privacy Policy

We don't collect personal data. Your privacy is our priority.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lottery Pattern Analyzer. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Service.

Our core privacy principle is simple: We don't collect your personal data. This policy explains what minimal information we might access and how we protect your privacy.

2. Information We DON'T Collect

To be perfectly clear, Lottery Pattern Analyzer does NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information: Name, email address, phone number, physical address
  • Financial information: Credit card numbers, bank account information, payment details
  • Location data: GPS coordinates, IP-based location tracking
  • Biometric data: Fingerprints, facial recognition data
  • Sensitive personal data: Race, ethnicity, political opinions, religious beliefs
  • Social media information: Social media profiles, contacts, connections
  • Health information: Medical history, health conditions

Important: We don't require accounts, registration, or any personal information to use our Service. Everything works anonymously.

3. Technical Information (Minimal)

When you use our Service, some technical information is automatically collected by your web browser and hosting provider. This information is standard for all websites and includes:

3.1 Server Logs

Our hosting provider may collect:

  • IP address (anonymized where possible)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referral source (what site brought you here)
  • Date and time of access

This information is used for:

  • Maintaining service security and stability
  • Diagnosing technical issues
  • Improving service performance
  • Preventing abuse and attacks

3.2 Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to store:

  • Your theme preference (light/dark mode)
  • Your selected lottery game settings
  • Your number generation history (stored locally on your device only)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you clear your browser cache.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal, essential cookies only:

  • No advertising cookies: We don't show ads, so no ad-tracking cookies
  • No analytics cookies: We don't use Google Analytics or similar tracking
  • No social media cookies: No Facebook, Twitter, or other social trackers
  • No third-party cookies: We don't embed content that sets third-party cookies

The only "cookies" we use are:

  • Session cookies: Temporary cookies that help the site function (deleted when you close your browser)
  • Preference cookies: Remember your theme and settings (optional)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

Locally on your device: Your preferences and generated numbers stay on your computer/phone.

Anonymous server logs: Temporarily stored on our secure servers and regularly purged.

5.2 Data Security

We implement security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure server infrastructure
  • Regular security updates
  • Minimal data collection (best security is not collecting data at all)

5.3 Data Retention

We don't retain personal data because we don't collect it. Server logs are typically retained for 30 days for security monitoring, then automatically deleted.

6. Third-Party Services

We don't share data with third parties because we don't collect personal data. However, note that:

  • Hosting Provider: Our hosting company has access to server logs (standard for all websites)
  • Email Service: If you contact us, your email goes through our email provider

We use privacy-focused, reputable providers who have their own privacy policies. We don't use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social media integrations.
