About Powerball

Powerball is an American lottery game offered by 45 states, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands. It is coordinated by the Multi-State Lottery Association (MUSL).

How to Play Powerball

To play Powerball, you need to select 5 numbers from 1 to 69 for the white balls, and 1 number from 1 to 26 for the red Powerball. You can choose your own numbers or use a Quick Pick.

Powerball Prizes

  • Jackpot: Match all 5 white balls and the Powerball
  • $1 Million: Match all 5 white balls
  • $50,000: Match 4 white balls and the Powerball
  • $100: Match 4 white balls or 3 white balls and the Powerball
  • $7: Match 3 white balls or 2 white balls and the Powerball
  • $4: Match 1 white ball and the Powerball or just the Powerball

Power Play

For an additional $1 per play, you can add Power Play to multiply non-jackpot prizes by 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, or 10x. The 10x multiplier is only available when the jackpot is $150 million or less.

About Mega Millions

Mega Millions is one of the two largest lottery jackpot games in the United States, along with Powerball. It is offered in 45 states, the District of Columbia, and the U.S. Virgin Islands.

How to Play Mega Millions

To play Mega Millions, select five numbers from 1 to 70 for the white balls, and one number from 1 to 25 for the gold Mega Ball. You can choose your own numbers or use a Quick Pick.

Mega Millions Prizes

  • Jackpot: Match all 5 white balls and the Mega Ball
  • $1 Million: Match all 5 white balls
  • $10,000: Match 4 white balls and the Mega Ball
  • $500: Match 4 white balls
  • $200: Match 3 white balls and the Mega Ball
  • $10: Match 3 white balls or 2 white balls and the Mega Ball
  • $4: Match 1 white ball and the Mega Ball or just the Mega Ball

Megaplier

For an additional $1 per play, you can add the Megaplier to multiply non-jackpot prizes by 2x, 3x, 4x, or 5x. The Megaplier number is drawn before each Mega Millions drawing from a pool of 15 balls.

About EuroMillions

EuroMillions is a transnational lottery that was launched in 2004 by France's Française des Jeux, Spain's Loterías y Apuestas del Estado, and the United Kingdom's Camelot. The game is available in nine European countries.

How to Play EuroMillions

To play EuroMillions, select five main numbers from 1 to 50 and two Lucky Star numbers from 1 to 12. You can choose your own numbers or use a Lucky Dip.

EuroMillions Prizes

  • Jackpot: Match 5 main numbers and 2 Lucky Stars
  • € Match 5 main numbers and 1 Lucky Star
  • € Match 5 main numbers
  • € Match 4 main numbers and 2 Lucky Stars
  • € Match 4 main numbers and 1 Lucky Star
  • € Match 3 main numbers and 2 Lucky Stars
  • € Match 4 main numbers
  • € Match 2 main numbers and 2 Lucky Stars
  • € Match 3 main numbers and 1 Lucky Star
  • € Match 3 main numbers
  • € Match 1 main number and 2 Lucky Stars
  • € Match 2 main numbers and 1 Lucky Star
  • € Match 2 main numbers

EuroMillions Countries

EuroMillions is available in Austria, Belgium, France, Ireland, Luxembourg, Portugal, Spain, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.
