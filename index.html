Lottery Probability Basics

Understanding probability is key to realistic expectations about lottery games:

  • Each number combination has an equal chance of being drawn
  • Past results don't influence future draws
  • Buying more tickets increases your chances, but the odds remain extremely low
  • Lotteries are designed as games of chance, not skill

Responsible Lottery Play

Always play responsibly and within your means:

  • Set a budget for lottery play and stick to it
  • View lottery tickets as entertainment, not investments
  • Never spend money you can't afford to lose
  • If playing becomes a problem, seek help from gambling support services
