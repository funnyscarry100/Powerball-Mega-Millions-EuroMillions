About Powerball

Powerball is one of America's favorite lottery games, known for its record-breaking jackpots. Players select five numbers from 1 to 69 and one Powerball number from 1 to 26.

How to Play

  • Select 5 numbers from 1-69 (white balls)
  • Select 1 number from 1-26 (red Powerball)
  • Matches are based on the numbers drawn in official Powerball drawings
  • Drawings are every Wednesday and Saturday at 10:59 PM ET
